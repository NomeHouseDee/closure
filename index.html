'New single' Closure out now (Released Oct. 2, 2026)

Moving the hearts of synth pop fans, using powerful vocals and a stadium-rocking saxophone solo, New York-based artist Breigh officially releases 'Closure', pairing her storytelling songwriting with genre-bending production from Juno-nominated producer Mike Schlosser. Closure pulls you into huge, heart-wrenching moments right when you lose yourself to the groove.

Breigh Hammel is a New York singer-songwriter who has spent the last three years focused on professionally recording and releasing singles, developing her own strategies for building a career around her passion for music and connecting with her fans. She's attracted 21K followers on Instagram, a private group chat with 700 active fans, and a generally dedicated fandom that actively sends her very touching messages.

“Closure took almost two years, two breakups, and countless versions with different producers to finally become what it is today. I wanted to make sure it truly captured the complicated emotions of losing someone I thought would be in my life forever. I'm endlessly grateful to every Slayer (group chat members) who stayed with me through the process and reminded me why I couldn't give up on this song. I truly couldn't have made it without you. <3” Breigh
